1. CHECK-IN PROCEDURE
Standard guest check-in time is 02:00 PM IST.
Standard check-out time is 11:00 AM IST.
Verify the guest reservation before completing check-in.
Confirm guest identity according to resort verification procedures.
Coordinate luggage assistance when requested.

2. BOOKING MANAGEMENT
Check reservation details before responding to booking questions.
Confirm room category, reservation dates, and booking status.
Do not promise a booking until it is confirmed in the reservation system.
Refer booking changes and cancellations to the authorized reservation process.

3. ROOM INFORMATION
Oceanfront Villa costs INR 48,000 per night plus 18% GST.
Room availability must be checked in the reservation system.
Do not invent room availability or pricing.

4. DINING SUPPORT
Fisherman's Cove lunch: 12:00 PM to 03:30 PM.
Fisherman's Cove dinner: 07:00 PM to 11:00 PM.
Assist guests with dining reservation enquiries.

5. GUEST SERVICE REQUESTS
Record guest requests with the correct service category.
Common service categories include Concierge and Spa Booking.
Track request status and coordinate with the responsible team.

6. ESCALATION
Unresolved guest complaints should be escalated to the appropriate supervisor.
For missing policy information, consult the authorized resort manager.
Never invent resort policies or confirm actions that have not been completed.
